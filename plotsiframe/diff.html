4. Dimensionality alone can't tell them apart

Both correlated and anticorrelated pair members settle at roughly the same average dimensionality (≈½, i.e. two features sharing one effective dimension) — because compute_dimensionality only sees the magnitude of interference, not its sign. You need Fig. 1's signed cosine to see that these are two very different geometric strategies (parallel vs. antipodal) achieving a similar 'capacity efficiency'.
