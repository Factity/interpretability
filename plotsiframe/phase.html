2. Per-feature dimensionality — the fractions are polytopes

Breaking D* down per individual feature reveals many more sticky fractions: ¾ (tetrahedron), ⅔ (triangle), ½ (antipodal pair), ⅖ (pentagon), ⅜ (square antiprism). Every point strictly between 0 and 1 is a feature in superposition — superposition is not one phenomenon but a whole family of geometric phases, the way 'ice' is really many different crystal structures.
